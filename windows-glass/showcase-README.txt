LIQUID GLASS FOR WINDOWS - Simpl's own glass, shown off
=======================================================

Run LiquidGlassShowcase.exe (one portable file). If Windows SmartScreen says
"Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway" (this test
build is not code-signed).

Try
  - Drag the "Unread announcements" popover over the coloured towers.
  - Click Cards / List / Activity and the tabs at the bottom: a lens slides.
  - Hold a switch's knob or the slider's: it turns into a lens of glass.
  - Drag the round bubble and fling it.
  - Tune (bottom right): frost, refraction, dispersion, bevel, colour, tint,
    rim; Dark or Light; the moving backdrop on or off. Its second line says
    whether the glass shader is running ("on") or the plainer fallback
    ("off": a PC or VM without a graphics card WPF can use).
