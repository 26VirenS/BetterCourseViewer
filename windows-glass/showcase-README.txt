LIQUID GLASS FOR WINDOWS - Simpl's showcase (native, Simpl's own glass)
=======================================================================

Run LiquidGlass.exe. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your
PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway" (this test build is not
code-signed).

Try
  - Compare the "Unread announcements" popover with the Mac's.
  - Tune > Corners: Round or Squircle (continuous corners) for every bar
    and card; the "Round" and "Squircle" pair shows both side by side.
  - Drag the popover and the panels around; drag the "Clear glass" card
    and the bubble (fling the bubble) over the backdrop.
  - Click Cards / List / Activity and the tabs at the bottom: a lens slides.
  - Hold a switch's knob or a slider's: it turns into a lens of glass.
  - Tune > Backdrop: Simpl (the Dashboard), Grid (a sharp test card) or
    Photo (your wallpaper and Windows' own photos; click Photo again for
    the next). Drop any picture on the window to see the glass over it.
  - Tune: Frost (0 = clear glass), Refraction, Edge, Dispersion, Colour,
    Tint, Rim; Dark or Light; the backdrop's motion.
  - The frame rate is in the window's title. Esc closes it.
