Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026.

Simpl Courses collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Everything stays on your device.

Nothing is collected No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
Nothing leaves your device Settings and grade history stay in your browser.
Your Canvas, your session Pages are read with the login you already have.
One site only Runs on the Canvas address you enter. Nowhere else.
Your own API key Stored locally. Sent only to your provider.
Nothing is sold There is no data to sell.

What we store, and where

Your settings and grade snapshots stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How Canvas data is read

It reads Canvas with the login you already have. It never sees your password.

Permissions

It runs only on the Canvas site you enter. It does not touch other sites or your history.

The optional smart panel

Off until you add your own API key. The key stays on your device and goes only to that provider.

Children and student records

It shows your own Canvas account. It creates no records and shares nothing with anyone.

Deleting your data

Remove the extension, or use Settings → Data → Reset everything. Nothing is held on a server.

Changes

If that ever changes, this page and the extension will say so.

Questions? Get in touch. Not affiliated with Instructure or Canvas.
