LIQUID GLASS FOR WINDOWS - Simpl's showcase (native, on AuroraGlass)
====================================================================

Keep the "shaders" folder beside LiquidGlass.exe, then run it. If Windows
SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
"Run anyway" (this test build is not code-signed).

Try
  - Drag the "Unread announcements" popover and the panels around.
  - Click Cards / List / Activity and the tabs at the bottom: a lens slides.
  - Hold a switch's knob or a slider's: it turns into a lens of glass.
  - Drag the "Clear glass" card and the round bubble (fling the bubble)
    over the grid's lines: see them bend at the rim.
  - Tune > Backdrop: Grid (a sharp test card), Photo (your wallpaper and
    Windows' own photos; click Photo again for the next) or Simpl.
    Drop any picture on the window to see the glass over it.
  - Tune: Frost (slide it to 0 for clear glass), refraction, dispersion,
    thickness, colour, tint, rim; Dark or Light; the backdrop's motion.
  - The frame rate is in the window's title. Esc closes it.

Glass: AuroraGlass (https://github.com/VeiQiuLab/AuroraGlass), Apache-2.0
