**Simpl for Windows, made again in Windows 11's look — an early preview.** A test build for evaluation, not a release: Simpl's own updates never offer it. The first Windows preview (windows-app-v…) stays available.

**Simpl-Windows-x64.zip** — unzip anywhere and run **Simpl.exe** (everything it needs is in the folder; it uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 10 and 11 already have). Not code-signed: on "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

What is in it so far:

- **Windows 11's own look**: Mica behind the window, rounded corners, the title bar with Back and snap layouts, Segoe UI Variable and Fluent icons, Windows 11's buttons, fields, toggles, cards and dialogs (the window dims behind each one), and Windows' motion;
- **finding your school and signing in, as on the Mac**: search for your school, see its sign-in page and confirm it, then sign in with Simpl's own card; "Logging you in", the note over a page you must finish yourself, Start Over, and "Stay logged in?" (kept in Windows' Credential Manager);
- **the app laid out like Windows 11 Settings**: your account at the top of the navigation pane (its menu: Settings, What's New, your profile, Report a Bug, Sign Out, Quit), the search box, Dashboard, To Do, Calendar, Grades, Notifications, Inbox, Tools, your courses with their sections, your groups; the page's title as a breadcrumb;
- **runs in the background**: closing the window keeps Simpl in the notification area (right-click its icon to quit);
- **the window's size**: it opens as you left it, never larger than your screen.

Screens Simpl does not draw itself yet open as your school's own page inside the window.
